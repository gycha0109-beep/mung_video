## Physical / visual limitations
- 20 panes, 17 breaks, 18th stops the bead using a simplified energy model.
- Animated snap/release and projectile are keyframed.
- Glass shattering is procedural, with rigid bodies for debris; not real fracture mechanics.
- Neutral studio lighting and thin materials are approximate, not photorealistic motion capture.
- Source Blender project should be rendered locally on Blender 5.2.3 LTS.
