# V5 single-pane quality benchmark

This is a **quality proof**, not a replacement for the complete V4 20-pane video.

- Scope: one glass sheet, one steel sphere impact, close-up continuous camera; 36 frames at 24 fps (1.5 seconds).
- Preview render: 504 × 896 (70% of 720 × 1280), Blender 5.2.3 LTS, Cycles CUDA.
- Visual rebuild: fully refractive glass with physical thickness and polished edges, ~96 irregular glass fragments, impact fissures, softer film lighting, physically modeled clamp blocks and concrete materials.
- Sound: licensed prerecorded Mixkit clips mixed into the MP4 using the local source assets from V4. Source sound files are not redistributed.
- Test: 36 source frames validated, H.264/AAC MP4 decoded end-to-end, the V4/V5 comparison page loaded both videos.

**Not resolved:** the fracture is artist-keyframed rather than a real stress simulation, and the distribution is still somewhat Voronoi-like. The result is not yet a confirmed photorealistic or full V5 product.

## Quality gate for next full release

1. A single impact must look credible at normal and slow playback.
2. Glass front/back/refraction and side thickness must be visible from the final camera angle.
3. Central micro-chips should lead the heavier outer pieces; the remaining pane must not pop out in a single frame.
4. Realistic mounts, materials, sky/background and lens exposure must remain coherent before/after contact.
5. The slingshot and launch must have reference-grade physical shape, tension and mechanical continuity.
6. Only after the one-pane benchmark passes should the scene be extended to all 20 panes and rendered at 720 × 1280.
